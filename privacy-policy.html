Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026 | AutoBoat.in

Welcome to AutoBoat.in ("AutoBoat", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of any personal and business data provided by our users and subscribers.

🔒 Core Commitment: AutoBoat.in operates strictly in full compliance with Meta’s Official WhatsApp Business API Policies, Information Technology Laws, and Data Protection Standards. We never sell, rent, or trade your lead data or business contacts to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our Official WhatsApp API, CRM, WhatsApp Mini-Apps, and In-Chat Payment SaaS services, including:

  • Account Information: Full name, business email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, and billing details when you sign up or request a demo.
  • WhatsApp Business API Data: Phone number associated with your Meta Business Account, template messages, and system webhook interactions processed through Meta Cloud API.
  • Lead & Customer Data: Customer contact details, conversation history, department assignments, and tags managed inside your AutoBoat CRM dashboard.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction logs with our site and web widgets.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for operational, functional, and security purposes:

  • To provision and manage your Official WhatsApp API Account and Meta Business Manager integration.
  • To execute broadcast campaigns, trigger automated chatbots, and render interactive WhatsApp Mini-Apps.
  • To facilitate native In-Chat payment links and automated GST invoice generation via secure payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, UPI).
  • To route leads efficiently across your designated Sales, Support, and Billing teams.
  • To send service notifications, security alerts, and customer support responses.

3. Data Protection & Security

AutoBoat employs 256-bit SSL encryption for data in transit and enterprise-grade encrypted storage for data at rest. Access to customer databases is restricted via strict role-based access controls (RBAC).

4. Third-Party Services & Meta Integration

AutoBoat integrates directly with Meta Platforms Inc. (Official Meta Cloud API) and secure payment gateways. Information shared with these providers is strictly governed by their respective privacy and data handling terms.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website utilizes essential cookies to maintain user sessions, enhance navigation, and analyze site performance. You may modify your browser settings to decline non-essential cookies.
